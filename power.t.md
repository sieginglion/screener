You’re right to challenge it. **I would revise the answer: the best single pick is 5269 祥碩 / ASMedia, not 4749 新應材.**

My earlier answer overweighted **upstream semiconductor-material qualification barriers**. That gives 新應材 pricing power, but it is not the cleanest *agentic-AI value-chain* bottleneck. Agentic AI is more directly constrained by **compute, data movement, networking, storage, and inference infrastructure**; NVIDIA describes AI data centers as full-stack accelerated computing platforms built on GPU, CPU, and networking architecture, and McKinsey describes infrastructure as the “backbone” of agentic AI. ([NVIDIA][1])

## Revised winner: 5269 祥碩 / ASMedia

**Why ASMedia has the strongest pricing power:**

ASMedia is a fabless high-speed interface IC company, and its AI relevance is improving through **PCIe packet switches, USB4, high-speed data transfer, edge AI, servers, storage, and AI development use cases**. At Computex 2025, ASMedia said its PCIe Packet Switch roadmap covers all PCIe generations, with 3–80 channel products for servers, edge computing, IPC, and NAS; it also said PCIe Gen5 and Gen6 products are on the roadmap. ([ASMedia][2])

The financial evidence is stronger than 新應材’s: ASMedia’s 2025 Q3 gross margin was **51.0%**, with operating margin around **29%** and net margin around **40%** in Q3. Its investor deck also shows long-term gross margin staying around the low-to-mid 50s despite technology migration. ([ASMedia][3])

That combination matters: **AI-chain relevance + IP ownership + high gross margin + technology migration = pricing power.**

## Why 新應材 moves to #2

新應材 is still a very strong pricing-power name. It supplies IC manufacturing materials including Positive PR, Polyimide, electronic-grade Developer, BARC, and advanced cleaning chemicals. ([AEMC][4]) Its gross margin is also high, around **44%**, and it ranks highly within Taiwan specialty chemicals. ([財報狗][5])

But after thinking again, I would not make it #1 because its pricing power is more **semiconductor-fab-chain pricing power** than **agentic-AI infrastructure pricing power**. It benefits from advanced-node demand, but it is further removed from the actual AI server / AI inference / data-movement bottleneck.
